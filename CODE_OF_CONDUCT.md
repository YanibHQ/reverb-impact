# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Reverb a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

Harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, threats, doxxing, and other conduct
that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to a YanibHQ organization owner. Maintainers will investigate
promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as possible, and take proportionate corrective action,
including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal. Maintainers who are the subject
of a report will not participate in its resolution.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
